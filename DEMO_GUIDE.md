# Hackathon demo guide (5 minutes)

**Setup:** set `OPS_USERNAME` and `OPS_PASSWORD` in the server environment (see README), run `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:4173`, and reset the demo. State up front that all sites, locations and operational figures are fictional.

1. **Discover (45 sec):** open Explore and tap **Use my location** (or choose a Mumbai area). Show the nearest MCGM artificial ponds on the map, select a row to fly to its pin, and open a site to show its MCGM source details. Point out that the list is 2025 data pending review.
   **Live status:** in a second window, sign in to Operations, post "Busy · 20 min" for that site, and show it appear as **Live** on the public page.
2. **Compare (30 sec):** open Lakeview Artificial Pond. Review facilities, arrival guidance and the simulated location notice.
3. **Reserve (75 sec):** choose a time, enter demo name/email and group size four. Confirm the persisted booking reference.
4. **Visit and support (60 sec):** open My booking, show confirmation and reference; request senior-citizen assistance and point out the returned request reference.
5. **Care and close (30 sec):** review waste guidance. Use reset to restore the demo.

For the staff view, choose **Staff sign in**, enter the configured credentials, and review the read-only operations site overview. Sign out when done. All values remain fictional demo data.

The displayed booking pass is illustrative only, not scan-ready. Queue numbers are fictional and are not refreshed from an operational system. Staff acknowledgement, QR scanning, navigation and visit completion are future work and must not be narrated as implemented.
